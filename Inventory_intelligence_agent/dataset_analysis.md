# Dataset Audit and Analysis: Inventory Planning Agent

This document summarizes the properties, schemas, volumes, and date horizons of the six key datasets located in the [Inventory_intelligence_agent/data](file:///c:/Users/GauryJithesh/Desktop/aarushi/IntelliSupply/Inventory_intelligence_agent/data) directory.

## 1. Dataset Breakdown

### 📊 `demand_forecast_daily.csv` (2.4 MB)
This dataset contains predicted future demands generated by machine learning models (e.g., Tuned XGBoost) for a 30-day forecast horizon.
- **Row Count**: 27,600 rows
- **Date Horizon**: `2024-01-31` to `2024-02-29`
- **Key Schema & Variables**:
  - `forecast_date` (str): Anchor date representing when the forecast begins.
  - `hub_id` (int64): Unique ID of the distribution warehouse center (e.g., `45`).
  - `product_id` (str): Product catalog reference identifier (e.g., `P0019`).
  - `category` (str): General inventory category (e.g., `Furniture`).
  - `forecast_horizon_day` (int64): Integer days into the future (1 to 30).
  - `predicted_demand` (int64): Predicted sales count for that specific day.
  - `lower_bound` / `upper_bound` (int64): Range bounds representing forecast uncertainty.
  - `confidence_score` (float64): ML confidence metrics (e.g., `50.0%`).
  - `generated_at` (str): Timestamp representing when the ML inference job ran.

---

### ⏱️ `replenishment_delay_stats.csv` (54 KB)
A small metadata statistical summary of average lead times and delay standard deviations per hub-product combination, used for Monte Carlo noise generation.
- **Row Count**: 920 rows
- **Key Schema & Variables**:
  - `hub_id` (int64) / `product_id` (str) / `category` (str)
  - `delay_mean` (float64): Historical average delay in delivery transit (in days).
  - `delay_std` (float64): Volatility standard deviation in transit days.
  - `sample_count` (int64): Total past orders observed to construct these metrics.

---

### 🚚 `replenishment_orders.csv` (8.9 MB)
Details the status, order size, and actual arrival dates of purchase replenishment shipments sent to hubs.
- **Row Count**: 93,321 rows
- **Date Horizon**: `2022-01-01` to `2024-01-30`
- **Key Schema & Variables**:
  - `replenishment_id` (str): Unique PO string (e.g., `RO0000001`).
  - `order_date` (str) / `expected_arrival_date` (str) / `actual_arrival_date` (str)
  - `quantity_ordered` (int64) / `quantity_received` (int64)
  - `lead_time_days` (int64) / `actual_delay_days` (int64)
  - `replenishment_status` (str): e.g., `Delivered`, `In Transit`.
  - `priority` (str): e.g., `Critical`, `High`, `Medium`, `Low`.

---

### 📈 `demand_analysis_daily.csv` (116 MB)
Historical sales records, promotional tags, and external conditions representing the demand signals.
- **Row Count**: 699,200 rows
- **Date Horizon**: `2022-01-01` to `2024-01-30`
- **Key Schema & Variables**:
  - `demand_date` (str) / `hub_id` (int64) / `product_id` (str)
  - `actual_demand` (int64): The actual customer order count.
  - `fulfilled_quantity` (int64): Fulfilled orders (constrained by inventory levels).
  - `lost_demand` (int64): Missed sales due to stockouts.
  - `rolling_7_avg_demand` / `rolling_30_avg_demand` (float64): Moving averages.
  - `demand_growth_pct` (float64): Current growth rate trend.
  - `yoy_trend_label` (str): Year-over-year growth status labels.
  - `demand_cv` (float64) / `volatility_label` (str): Volatility of demand signals.
  - `price` (float64) / `discount` (int64) / `promotion` (int64): Pricing variables.
  - `competitor_pricing` (float64) / `weather_condition` (str) / `seasonality` (str) / `epidemic` (int64)

### 🏥 `inventory_health_daily.csv` (97 MB)
Main dataset representing historical inventory levels, stockout risks, safety thresholds, and turnover ratios.
- **Row Count**: 699,200 rows
- **Date Horizon**: `2022-01-01` to `2024-01-30`
- **Key Schema & Variables**:
  - `health_date` (str) / `hub_id` (int64) / `product_id` (str)
  - `current_stock` (int64): Available stock on hand.
  - `days_of_inventory_remaining` / `coverage_days` (float64): Estimated survival days of current stock.
  - `safety_stock` (int64) / `threshold_quantity` (int64) / `threshold_gap` (int64): Tracking safety margins.
  - `threshold_status` (str): e.g., `Below Threshold`, `Above Threshold`.
  - `inventory_status` (str): e.g., `Critical Stockout Risk`, `High Stockout Risk`, `Healthy`.

### 🚨 `inventory_risk_daily.csv` (177 MB)
Granular breakdown of risk levels and risk score components per hub-product combination.
- **Row Count**: 699,200 rows
- **Date Horizon**: `2022-01-01` to `2024-01-30`
- **Key Schema & Variables**:
  - `risk_date` (str) / `hub_id` (int64) / `product_id` (str)
  - `stock_coverage_risk` (int64) / `demand_volatility_risk` (int64) / `seasonality_risk` (int64) / `replenishment_delay_risk` (int64): Multi-dimensional risk scoring.
  - `composite_risk_score` (float64): Weighted composite risk index (0 to 100).
  - `risk_level` (str): e.g., `High`, `Medium`, `Low`.
  - `primary_risk_driver` (str): e.g., `Stock Coverage Risk`.

## 2. Core Observations & Implications

1. **Volume & Density**:
   - The dataset represents **46 Hubs** (IDs `0` through `45`) and **100 Products** (`P0001` through `P0100`) across **5 Categories** (`Electronics`, `Clothing`, `Groceries`, `Toys`, `Furniture`).
   - The historical daily datasets cover exactly **760 days** of snapshots per hub-product pair (699,200 records $\div$ 920 active hub-product-category keys = 760 days).
2. **Offline Date Horizon Constraints**:
   - The historical records and orders all end on **`2024-01-30`**, with forecasts anchored on the date **`2024-01-31`**. This means only dates on or before `2024-01-31` are valid query keys. Sending a simulation date of `2026` or today's date will not match any historical data context.
3. **Statistical Noise modeling**:
   - By feeding `replenishment_delay_stats.csv` and the demand std deviation (`demand_cv`) into the generator, the Monte Carlo pipeline is able to simulate custom world instances (e.g., best/worst case delays) that are highly statistically accurate relative to historical variances.
